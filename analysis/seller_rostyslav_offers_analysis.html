🛡️ Buyer's Field Testing Protocol (Metro Służew)

Because Rostyslav operates strictly via in-person cash transactions without OLX Safe Buy protection, follow this step-by-step testing procedure before handing over payment:

STEP 01
Prepare Testing USB Drive

Load a portable flash drive with HWiNFO64, FurMark 2, CrystalDiskInfo, and KeyboardTestUtility (no internet connection needed).

STEP 02
5-Minute FurMark GPU Stress

Run FurMark at native 1080p for 5 minutes. Watch for GPU artifacts (pink/green checkerboards), sudden black screens, or thermal throttling above 86°C.

STEP 03
SSD Wear & Power-On Hours

Open CrystalDiskInfo. Verify total power-on hours and health status. Less than 2,000 hours indicates light usage; over 10,000 hours suggests heavy refurbished origin.

STEP 04
Battery Wear Report

Open PowerShell and run powercfg /batteryreport. Compare "Full Charge Capacity" against "Design Capacity". Reject units degraded below 60%.

STEP 05
Inspect Keyboard & Hinge Action

Check key legends for localized stickers (spolszczające naklejki) indicating European imports. Open and close the screen twice to verify smooth hinge anchors.

STEP 06
Original AC Power Adapter

Ensure the charger is the original manufacturer brick (e.g. 170W Lenovo / 200W HP), not a cheap universal 65W charger that will throttle the GPU during gaming.
